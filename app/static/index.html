Pre-Visit Intake

This is a simulated patient intake. Click Start intake and the assistant will walk you through the chief complaint, history of present illness, and a targeted review of systems. At the end, it generates a structured clinical brief.

Voice input requires Chrome or Edge. If your browser doesn't support it, just type — the agent works the same way.

How this works

  1. Click Start intake.
  2. Answer the agent's questions one at a time, by voice or by text.
  3. Click End intake when you're done. We generate a structured brief and open it.

The agent flags red-flag symptoms (chest pain with radiation, stroke signs, etc.) in real time and instructs you to seek emergency care. It does not diagnose or give medical advice — it gathers structured information for the clinician.
